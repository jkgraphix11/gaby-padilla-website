What does the bridal package include?

Makeup and hair trial, Hydrafacial + microdermabrasion facial, facial hair removal and eyebrow shaping, and wedding-day makeup and hairstyling.

Can the trial be used for engagement or pre-wedding photos?

Yes. The trial can be planned around your pre-wedding photos, depending on scheduling availability.

Is airbrush makeup included?

Yes. You can choose airbrush makeup as part of the bridal package at no extra cost.

Do you provide hair extensions?

If you already own clip-in extensions, installation is included. Hair extensions themselves are not included.

The total is $12,000 MXN. Reserve with $2,000 MXN and pay the remaining $10,000 MXN in two installments. Confirm payment due dates when booking.

Do you travel to Valle de Guadalupe wedding venues?

On-location services in Valle de Guadalupe and other Tijuana areas may be available. Travel, timing and any additional charges are confirmed separately.
