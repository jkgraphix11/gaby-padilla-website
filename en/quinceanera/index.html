Can you travel to San Diego for weddings and events?

Absolutely! I welcome clients visiting Tijuana from San Diego. I can also travel to San Diego for weddings and special events on request, subject to availability. Additional fees may apply. Please message me on WhatsApp before booking to confirm your location, timing and travel quote.

Do you offer wedding and event makeup in Tecate and Valle de Guadalupe?

Yes. Gaby offers on-location makeup and hairstyling for weddings, quinceañeras and special events in Tecate and Valle de Guadalupe. Contact us before booking to confirm availability, timing, travel and any additional charges.

What is included in the quinceañera package?

A hair and makeup trial, Hydrafacial + microdermabrasion, facial hair removal, eyebrow shaping and event-day makeup and hairstyling.

Can the trial be used for pre-event pictures?

Yes. You may be able to schedule the trial alongside your pre-event photos, subject to availability.

Are hair extensions included?

Installation of your own clip-in extensions is included, but hair extensions themselves are not supplied.

What is the price and deposit?

The package is approx. $428.56 USD. A approx. $107.14 USD deposit holds your date and the remaining approx. $321.42 USD can be paid in two installments. Confirm due dates when reserving.

When will the skin treatments be scheduled?

The facial and facial-hair and eyebrow preparation are coordinated in advance, generally around a week before the event, depending on your skin’s needs.

Where are appointments held?

The private studio is in Playas de Tijuana. Please ask about travel and availability if you need event-day services elsewhere.
