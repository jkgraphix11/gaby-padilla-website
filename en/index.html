Yes. Gaby offers on-location makeup and hairstyling for weddings, quinceañeras and special events in Tecate and Valle de Guadalupe. Contact us before booking to confirm availability, timing, travel and any additional charges.

How do I book an appointment?

Browse the services, pick an available time, and book directly through AgendaPro using any “Book now” button on this page.

Where are you located?

My studio is based in Playas de Tijuana, Baja California. Visits are by appointment only, and the exact location is shared after your booking is confirmed.

Do bridal and quinceañera packages include a trial?

Yes. Both packages include a makeup and hair trial, combination facial, facial hair removal, and event-day makeup and hairstyling.

Absolutely! I welcome clients visiting Tijuana from San Diego. I can also travel to San Diego for weddings and special events on request, subject to availability. Additional fees may apply. Please message me on WhatsApp before booking to confirm your location, timing and travel quote.
